----------------------------Story behind this Project and conclusion of research------------------------------

This is a game that was made to test the capabilities and how to best optimize the use of AI in a game development cycle.

The conclusion of this research is that it best serves as a tool for the user to use at their discretion. You could use it to
develop the full game, but there's no fun in that, so instead I used it to speed up my research into the other tools and classes that I used
in this game (for example, GameWindow), and figured out how to best utilize them. 

AI is a tool that can give you information on other tools, but it can also serve to help someone find a starting point 
when they may not have all the information available to them. 

These are the two main ways I would recommend using A.I in the development process, as I figured out through this project.

-------------------------------How to play APASTRON and run the project---------------------------------------

Since there is no executable file or anything like that:

1. Download all the files and store them in a specific place.
2. Open them using a JAVA IDE of your choosing (I personally used JGRASP for this, but you can use IntelliJ as far as I'm aware)
3. Run the code by selecting the run button in your IDE, from there a window should pop up and the game will start.

------------------------------------------How to play the GAME--------------------------------------------

Goal: Survive against an onslaught of different enemies and hazards such as asteroids

===============Controls===============

WASD to move.
Move your mouse to aim your ship.
left-click to fire your weapon.
right-click to launch a supernova bomb (there is a cooldown between uses)

+++++++++++++++++++++++++Enemies+++++++++++++++++++++++++++

Fighter - This spaceship will approach you and fire bullets at you dealing damage to you.

Stabber - This spaceship will approach you and then proceed to charge at you in an attempt to stab your ship (hence the name) dealing damage to you.

Drainer (Implemented kind of but very buggy) - will approach you and attempt to grab your ship in order to slowly drain you of life, 
it has more hp than other enemies understanding that it will be shot in the process.

Healer (Not implemented) - Will heal nearby enemy ships from any damage you deal and does so quickly, does not attack you directly.
